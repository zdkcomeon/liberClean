# LiberClean logo

- Generation: built-in imagegen tool.
- Plugin asset: `logo.png`, 256 × 256 pixels, PNG.
- Favicon: `public/favicon.png`, 64 × 64 pixels, PNG.
- The generated image is resized for delivery with macOS `sips`.

## Final prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create a brand-new application icon for LiberClean, a macOS cleaning and storage maintenance plugin for uTools. One centered, distinctive geometric LC monogram: a bold white L with its lower stroke flowing into a cyan open C-shaped clean sweep, plus one small four-point cyan sparkle in the upper right. Match the project's restrained cool-blue interface. Full-bleed deep navy square background (#141b26), white main mark, light cyan accent. Crisp, minimal, flat vector-like rendering, balanced negative space, strong silhouette and thick shapes that remain recognizable at 24 and 32 pixels. The emblem should occupy about 72 percent of the square. A single finished logo only, no wordmark, no captions, no watermark, no mockup, no sheet of variants, no mascot, no detailed broom or trash can, no thin lines, no 3D effects, no outer white margin. Square PNG for the final plugin asset at 256 by 256 pixels.
```
